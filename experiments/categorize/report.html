Mercor is assembling a panel of chemistry and chemical safety experts to red-team frontier AI models. The goal is to test whether a model can correctly judge the misuse potential of a technical request — answering legitimate questions fully while refusing genuinely dangerous ones.

You will:

Role Title: Aircraft Pilot

Role Type: Contractor

Location: Remote

micro1 is engaging professional Aircraft Pilots to contribute to a customer's innovative project focused on advancing AI in aviation. In this role, you'll apply your expertise to help train next-generation AI systems. Your work will shape how models learn, reason, and perform through high-quality, real-world input. No prior experience in AI is required — your domain knowledge is what matters.

Scope of Work

- Listen to authentic air traffic control (ATC) radio transmissions and accurately transcribe audio following detailed project guidelines

- Extract and document key flight instructions, including headings, altitudes, and radio frequencies

- Reference supporting materials such as airport diagrams, navigational charts, and aircraft positioning data to ensure precise transcription and annotation

- Apply aviation experience to interpret diverse communication styles, accents, and operational contexts commonly encountered in real-world ATC

- Meet a rigorous standard for transcription accuracy, targeting a 2% word error rate or better

- Contribute feedback for ongoing improvements to guidelines, processes, and AI understanding of ATC communications

Preferred Qualifications

- Active or former professional pilot with substantial experience in real-world flight operations

- Fluency in English, with proven ability to interpret and communicate complex ATC instructions

- Experience operating internationally and understanding a variety of regional and non-native English accents

- Exceptional attention to detail, with the ability to follow structured annotation and transcription protocols

- Comfort using digital tools, reference materials, and high-quality audio equipment in a remote setting

- Reliable internet connection and a quiet workspace equipped with good headphones

- Prior experience with data annotation, transcription, or similar structured data projects is a plus
